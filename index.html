Our story

Every December, Alene DeBrito could be found in her kitchen dipping dried apricots in chocolate. They were gifts for the friends, employees and customers of her husband Arnold's farm equipment business. People kept asking for more, and the kitchen turned into a candy factory.

When Alene retired in 2016, she handed the business to her longtime employee Tina Hernandez and Tina's husband, Juan. They kept it running for eight years, through the pandemic and with a small crew.

Juan passed away in June 2024. Before he did, he trusted the shop to his son Patrick, daughter-in-law Beth and wife Tina. Today the three of them keep making chocolates the way the DeBritos always have: by hand, for the people of Hollister and beyond.
